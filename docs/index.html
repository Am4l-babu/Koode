How it works

Four clear steps from a verified need to a delivered donation.

Verified request

Organisations post specific requirements, and our team confirms they are genuine.

Choose

Browse current needs and select an item you are able to provide.

Give

Confirm items and quantities, and describe what you're giving — condition, size or pack, a photo. We reserve the quantity, so a need is never over-committed.

Deliver

Our team coordinates every leg of the handover; no names are exchanged. Sending by courier? Add the tracking number and the organisation can follow it.
